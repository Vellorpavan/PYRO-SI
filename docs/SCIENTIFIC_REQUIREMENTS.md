## 1. Principles of Scientific Honesty & Data Contracts

In aerospace life support and spacecraft fire safety engineering, misrepresenting an exploratory hypothesis as an experimental law or claiming an uncalibrated model has "$\pm 14\%$ uncertainty" is unacceptable. GODEYE // PYRO-SI enforces strict scientific data contracts for every value, chart, and statement in the application:

### 1.1. Epistemic Classification Hierarchy
- **`MEASURED`**: An authentic numerical measurement recorded by flight instrumentation during an orbital experiment (e.g., calibrated thermocouple logs from SAFFIRE-I `PSI-98`, droplet diameter decay rates from FLEX `PSI-69`).
- **`REPORTED`**: Stated in an official NASA investigation record, Technical Memorandum (TM), or peer-reviewed flight report (e.g., fuel dimensions, cabin ambient conditions, mission dates).
- **`DERIVED`**: Deterministically calculated from identified measurements using standard physical laws (e.g., computing oxygen partial pressure $p\text{O}_2 = P_{\text{total}} \times Y_{\text{O}_2}$ via Dalton's Law, or orthogonal vector velocity $v_{\text{eff}} = \sqrt{v_f^2 + v_b^2}$).
- **`MODELLED`**: Generated by a documented theoretical equation, empirical correlation, or 1D phenomenological proxy (e.g., classical De Ris thin-fuel energy balance predictions). Must clearly state assumptions, limitations, and single-point calibration baselines.
- **`EXPLORATORY`**: Continuous mathematical interpolation across parameter spaces lacking long-duration flight verification (e.g., the partial-gravity flame spread curve based on T'ien / Ferkul low-buoyancy theory).
- **`ILLUSTRATIVE`**: Visual or educational representation designed to communicate physical morphology (e.g., Three.js 3D meshes, particle shaders). Explicitly NOT a Navier-Stokes Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) calculation.
- **`UNKNOWN`**: Conditions where no reliable flight or ground experimental data exists in the public domain (e.g., flame propagation across electrostatic lunar regolith dust mixtures).

## 4. Partial-Gravity & Microgravity Combustion Science

### 4.1. Conceptual Decomposition of Combustion Transport Mechanisms
Combustion dynamics in reduced and microgravity require strict conceptual separation of transport mechanisms:
1. **Buoyant Convection ($V_{\text{buoyant}}$):** Natural convection draft caused by gravity acting on density gradients:
   $$V_{\text{buoyant}} \approx 32.0 \cdot \sqrt{\frac{g}{1.0}}\text{ cm/s}$$
   Zero in microgravity ($0\text{g}$); $32\text{ cm/s}$ at $1\text{g}$; $45.25\text{ cm/s}$ in $2\text{g}$ hypergravity.
2. **Forced Convection ($V_{\text{forced}}$):** Imposed mechanical ventilation draft from cabin environmental control fans.
3. **Molecular Diffusion Transport ($D_{\text{molecular}}$):** Fickian mass transfer of oxygen into the reaction zone and combustion products outward. Governs flame persistence in quiescent microgravity ($V_{\text{effective}} = 0$).
4. **Radiative & Conductive Heat Losses ($Q_{\text{loss}}$):** In quiescent zero-g, long residence times allow radiative heat loss from soot and combustion products ($CO_2$, $H_2O$) to dominate, dictating spherical flame extinction or cool-flame persistence (500–800 K).

### 4.2. Zero-Gravity Model Domain Governance
- **No Automatic Extinction Fallacy:** It is scientifically incorrect to claim that zero gravity and zero forced flow ($V_{\text{effective}} = 0$) results in automatic flame extinction. Authentic NASA flight data from gaseous spherical diffusion flames (ACME s-Flame) and isolated liquid droplets (FLEX PSI-69) proves that diffusion-driven combustion can persist in microgravity without forced flow. Crucially, s-Flame and FLEX demonstrate distinct combustion modes (gaseous burner vs liquid droplet evaporation/$d^2$ law/cool flames) and are not interchangeable evidence.
- **Model Scope Boundary & Computational Guardrails:** The 1D De Ris energy balance proxy is derived strictly for convective boundary layers ($V_{\text{effective}} \ge 0.5\text{ cm/s}$). The thresholds $0.5\text{ cm/s}$ and $1.0\text{ cm/s}$ are project-defined computational guardrails, not universal physical extinction boundaries. When $g < 0.001$ and $V_{\text{forced}} < 0.5\text{ cm/s}$, the model returns an explicit **`MODEL_OUT_OF_SCOPE`** status. Crucially, `MODEL_OUT_OF_SCOPE` denotes mathematical inapplicability of the 1D convective boundary layer proxy; it does NOT imply physical flame extinction, nor does it guarantee indefinite persistence.
- **Microgravity ($\sim 10^{-6}\text{g}$):** Comprehensively investigated on the Space Shuttle and ISS (CIR and MSG facilities). In this regime, buoyant natural convection is suppressed, and transport is dominated by pure molecular diffusion and forced ventilation drafts ($10 - 20\ \text{cm/s}$).
- **Partial Gravity ($0.166\text{g}$ Moon, $0.38\text{g}$ Mars):** Almost zero archived flight data exists in PSI. Parabolic flights provide only $\sim 20$ seconds of low-quality, g-jittered partial gravity. Long-duration surface fire behavior in lunar or martian exploration atmospheres remains an active, unverified frontier.

### 6.4. De Ris (1969) Analytical Formulation & Model Attribution
- **Authoritative Citation:** J.N. De Ris, *"Spread of a laminar diffusion flame"*, *Twelfth Symposium (International) on Combustion*, The Combustion Institute, Pittsburgh, PA, 1969, pp. 241–252, DOI: `10.1016/S0082-0784(69)80407-8`.
- **Original De Ris (1969) Closed-Form Formulations:**
  - **Thermally Thin Solid Regime ($\text{Bi} \ll 1$):**
    $$V_f = \frac{\sqrt{2} \lambda_g}{\rho_s c_s \tau} \left(\frac{T_f - T_v}{T_v - T_\infty}\right)$$
    where $\tau$ is the solid half-thickness, $\rho_s c_s \tau$ represents the solid thermal capacity per unit area, and $\lambda_g$ is gas thermal conductivity. The spread velocity is inversely proportional to solid thickness and linear in the driving temperature difference. **Status:** Designated **`UNVERIFIED [LITERATURE FORMULATION NOT INSPECTED IN PRIMARY SOURCE]`**. **Crucially, this formulation is NOT used as a calculation path in the application.**
  - **Thermally Thick Solid Regime ($\text{Bi} \gg 1$):**
    $$V_f = V_a \frac{\lambda_g \rho_g c_{pg}}{\lambda_s \rho_s c_{ps}} \left(\frac{T_f - T_v}{T_v - T_\infty}\right)^2$$
    where $V_a$ is the opposed flow velocity, independent of thickness, and quadratic in the temperature difference. **Status:** Designated **`UNVERIFIED [LITERATURE FORMULATION NOT INSPECTED IN PRIMARY SOURCE]`**. **This formulation is NOT used as a calculation path in the application.**
- **Radiation Treatment in De Ris (1969):**
  - **Correction:** Radiation is explicitly included in the original 1969 formulation as a heat loss at the flame sheet and a heat gain at the fuel-bed surface (as stated directly in the original abstract). Radiation is **NOT** neglected in De Ris's theoretical model.
- **Runtime Model Input Contract & Equation Reconciliation:**
  - The actual implemented code in `physics-model.js` computes:
    $$\text{spreadIndex} = \max\left(0.0,\ \left(\frac{Y_{\text{O}_2} - \text{loi}}{21.0 - 15.5}\right)^{1.35} \times \left(\frac{P}{10.2}\right)^{0.35} \times \text{ventBonus}(v_{\text{eff}})\right)$$
    where:
    1. **Oxygen Concentration ($Y_{\text{O}_2}$):** Input key `o2` in volume/mole percent. Clamped to lower floor `0.0%`. When $Y_{\text{O}_2} \le \text{loi}$, the term $(Y_{\text{O}_2} - \text{loi})/5.5$ is clamped to software floor $0.1$ by `Math.max(0.1, ...)`, yielding a base $0.1^{1.35} \approx 0.0447$. The residual non-zero `spreadIndex` is a **software implementation constraint**, not an experimental extinction limit. (Material-specific rules apply: Nomex is clamped to $0.0\text{ mm/s}$ below $27.5\%\ \text{O}_2$; SIBAL is clamped to $0.2\text{ mm/s}$ in hypoxic purge $<10\%\ \text{O}_2$).
    2. **Limiting Oxygen Index ($\text{loi}$):** Input in volume percent $\text{O}_2$. Scaled via $\text{loi} = \text{clamp}((17.5 + \text{flowFactor}) \times (14.7/P)^{0.18}, 12.5, 24.0)$. Classified as `UNVERIFIED_MODELLED_HEURISTIC`.
    3. **Pressure ($P$):** Input key `psi` in psia. Benchmark references: $10.2\text{ psia}$ in `pressScale` and $14.7\text{ psia}$ in `pressureFactor`. Positive pressures $<0.1\text{ psia}$ clamp to software floor $0.1\text{ psia}$. At hard vacuum ($P = 0$), `pressScale = 0.0`, `spreadIndex = 0.0`, and risk score clamps to software floor $8\%$.
    4. **Ventilation ($v_{\text{forced}}$):** Input key `flow` in $\text{cm/s}$. Evaluated piecewise: $v_{\text{eff}} < 2.0\text{ cm/s} \rightarrow 0.35$; $2.0 \le v_{\text{eff}} \le 22.0\text{ cm/s} \rightarrow 1.25$; $v_{\text{eff}} > 22.0\text{ cm/s} \rightarrow 0.95$. Discontinuous software heuristic.
    5. **Gravity ($g$):** Input key `g` in Earth gravities ($g/g_0$). Gravity enters `spreadIndex` **SOLELY through buoyant velocity** $v_{\text{buoyant}} = 32.0\sqrt{g}\text{ cm/s}$ coupling orthogonally into $v_{\text{eff}} = \sqrt{v_{\text{forced}}^2 + v_{\text{buoyant}}^2}$. Gravity does **NOT** enter via an independent exponent. In zero-g ($g = 0$), $v_{\text{buoyant}} = 0$, so $v_{\text{eff}} = v_{\text{forced}}$. Quiescent zero-g ($g < 0.001$, flow $< 0.5\text{ cm/s}$) triggers `MODEL_OUT_OF_SCOPE`.
  - **Epistemic Classification of Exponents:** Exponents ($1.35$, $0.35$, piecewise vent tiers) are classified strictly as **`UNVERIFIED_MODELLED_HEURISTIC`**. They possess **zero independent multi-variable experimental flight validation** and are retained solely as an uncalibrated 1D exploratory proxy anchored to single-point SAFFIRE-I SIBAL data ($2.24\text{ mm/s}$ at $20\text{ cm/s}$, $21.5\%\ \text{O}_2$).
- **Original Analytical Assumptions (7 Formulations):**
  1. Steady, two-dimensional laminar opposed flow over a semi-infinite planar fuel bed.
  2. Fuel is categorized as either thermally thin ($\text{Bi} \ll 1$) or thermally thick ($\text{Bi} \gg 1$).
  3. One-step gas-phase chemical reaction with infinite reaction rate (flame sheet approximation).
  4. Constant and uniform thermophysical properties in each phase ($\lambda, \rho, c_p$).
  5. Uniform opposed oxidizer velocity $V_a$ (Oseen boundary-layer flow approximation).
  6. Radiation is explicitly included as heat loss at the flame sheet and heat gain at the fuel-bed surface (NOT neglected).
  7. Solid fuel pyrolyzes at a single discrete vaporization temperature $T_v$.
- **Project-Defined Computational Guardrails:**
  - Cutoff floor of $0.5\text{ cm/s}$ (sub-convective regime where 1D boundary layer assumptions collapse).
  - Buoyant draft floor of $1.0\text{ cm/s}$ for low-g numerical stability.
  - Designated explicitly as `PROJECT_DEFINED_MODEL_GUARDRAILS` in code and UI, never confused with universal physical extinction constants.
